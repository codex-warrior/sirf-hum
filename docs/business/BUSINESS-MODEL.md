# Business Model

Status: **Direction set (Prompt 01). Pricing is a hypothesis, not a decision.**
Nothing here is implemented; no prices are hard-coded anywhere.

## Primary model: subscription, per couple

### Free

Core relationship functionality stays genuinely useful without payment.
Potential free scope: couple account, messaging, basic calls, basic
Together, basic Moments, basic Future, basic rituals, **all core privacy
and security**. The free product is not artificially crippled.

### Sirf Hum Plus

A richer experience, not a ransom. Potential premium scope: expanded
encrypted media storage, advanced Together experiences, premium games,
advanced memory organisation, enhanced Future features, couple
customisation, premium themes and reactions, special experiences, future
privacy-respecting AI-assisted experiences.

**One subscription covers both partners.** The product is not designed
around charging each partner independently. (Implementation must handle:
who pays, what happens on unpairing, cross-platform entitlements across
Google Play, App Store and web.)

### Pricing hypotheses (not locked)

- ₹99–₹199 per couple / month
- ₹799–₹1,499 per couple / year

## Advertising

Sirf Hum is **not** designed around behavioural advertising. We will not
monetise private conversations, relationship information, private memories
or personal emotional data.

## Possible future revenue (documented, not implemented)

1. Couple subscriptions (primary)
2. Premium digital experiences
3. Premium themes / customisation
4. Carefully selected partner experiences
5. Regulated financial partnerships (**Our Money** — requires separate
   legal, compliance, security and regulatory architecture; never an
   unregulated investment product; never fake investment features)
6. Future commerce opportunities

## Monetization ethics

- Basic privacy is never a paid feature.
- Users never pay to access their own private relationship data.
  Exceeding storage limits must never lock users out of existing content.
- No emotional pressure. Never *"Your relationship is missing out unless
  you upgrade."* Premium reads as *"Here's a richer experience."*
- No dark patterns: clear pricing, easy cancellation, no hidden renewals.
