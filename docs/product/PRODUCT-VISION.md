# Product Vision

## The vision

> **Sirf Hum is the private digital home for two people.**

Couples today live across a dozen apps: one for messages, one for calls,
one for photos, a shared calendar, a notes app, a streaming service. None
of them is *theirs*. Their shared history is scattered, mixed in with
everyone else, and usually readable by the companies that host it.

Sirf Hum gives two people one private place to:

- **communicate** — with each other and nobody else;
- **spend time together** — even when apart;
- **create memories** — that stay theirs;
- **discover each other** — through rituals and questions;
- **plan their future** — dates, trips, dreams, milestones;
- **eventually build their life together.**

## Who it is for

Couples. Both people are first-class users with their own identity,
devices and privacy. The product must work for people with no technical
knowledge, across Android, iOS and the web.

The specific initial market, age ranges and relationship stages are **not
yet decided** (see Open decisions in the [Master Blueprint](../MASTER-BLUEPRINT.md)).
Pricing hypotheses in rupees suggest India as an early market; this is a
hypothesis, not a decision.

## Why people return

The core loop — connect, spend time together, create memories, plan the
future, grow together, return — compounds. Every call, moment and plan
adds to a private history that becomes more valuable the longer a couple
uses Sirf Hum. That value must never be held hostage: couples can always
access and export their own data (see
[BUSINESS-MODEL.md](../business/BUSINESS-MODEL.md#monetization-ethics)).

## What success feels like

- Opening Sirf Hum feels like coming home, not opening a tool.
- A first-time user pairs with their partner and does something together
  within minutes, without help.
- Couples trust Sirf Hum with things they would not put anywhere else —
  because Sirf Hum is built so it cannot read them.

## Long-term ambition

> **Every couple should have a Sirf Hum account.**

This demands consumer-grade simplicity, a free product that is genuinely
useful, privacy that is real rather than marketed, and a business model
that does not depend on exploiting relationship data.
